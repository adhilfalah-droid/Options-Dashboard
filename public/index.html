Calls and puts are never summed — each strike shows two separate bars, one per contract, reflecting contracts currently open right now. (24h volume isn't shown here — it's venue-specific and doesn't reconcile across exchanges the way open interest does.)

Max Pain Price is the settlement price at which option writers, in aggregate, owe the least to option holders — i.e. where Total Intrinsic Value (the dotted red curve, right axis) is lowest. It's a positioning read, not a prediction. Notional Value is total open interest × current spot. Since every figure here — spot included — is read from one venue, Notional Value is only ever expressed on that venue's own spot price; if you compare it against a figure computed on another exchange's spot, expect a small gap from the normal cross-venue price spread, not a data error.

OI Δ (24h) compares current open interest against a snapshot taken by this site roughly a day ago — it shows "Building history…" until enough snapshots exist to compare against, which is expected on a freshly deployed instance and not a bug.
